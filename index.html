Three steps to a finished assessment

  1. 1

    Describe the task

    Type what the crew will be doing, for example "welding on a scaffold platform near the crusher". Add the site if you want it on the document.

  2. 2

    Get a draft in your format

    Hazards, likelihood, severity, base risk, controls and residual risk come back filled in, using your own columns and rating scale.

  3. 3

    Review, edit, sign

    Download the Word or Excel file, adjust it with your team and sign it off. The judgement stays with your competent person.

Your template, filled in

Most tools make you move to their layout. This one fills yours. Upload the risk assessment your mine or company already uses and Pro Active reads its headers and completes it.

  • Works with Word (.docx) and Excel (.xlsx) templates, including Issue Based Risk Assessment layouts.
  • Understands abbreviated columns such as L, S, base risk (BR) and residual risk (RR).
  • Numbers the rows and fills the scope, date and reference number.
  • Downloads as a new file. Your original template is never changed.

What safety teams say

Questions

Does the AI replace our competent person?

No. Every assessment is a draft. A competent person must review, correct and approve it before anyone relies on it. The app is there to remove the blank page and the typing.

Can I use my own template?

Yes. Upload a Word or Excel risk assessment and the app fills it in. You can also use the built-in format if you don't have one.

How do my colleagues get access?

The account owner adds team members by email from inside the app, up to the user limit on your plan. Each person signs in with their own login.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancellation and refund terms are in our Terms and Conditions.

Does it work offline?

Not yet. Offline mode is planned for the Unlimited plan and will be made available as it is released.
